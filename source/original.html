What is a heat pump?
Heat pumps are reversible air conditioners that can cool your home in the summer, and heat your home in the winter - all from the same device. They work with and without central air ducts. If you have pre-existing central air, you'll probably end up swapping your furnace out with a heat pump air handler. If you have radiators and a boiler, we recommend going ductless in most cases.
Should I get a heat pump?
It really depends on what you're looking for, and where you live. If you don't have existing AC, heat pumps are an easy way to get air conditioning. If have a really old furnace or boiler, heat pumps can be an easy way to replace your system & get Mass Save incentives for doing so. Under January 2026 fuel prices, the cost to heat with a heat pump is about the same as a new oil and gas system (even with the seasonal heat pump discount), so the primary benefit here is sustainability, comfort, and AC.
How do you know what my home needs?
Because we will ask you a lot of questions on our system design intake form. Based on your responses, our algorithm will spit out a first-pass estimate tailored to your home & existing heating/AC system. If first pass looks okay to you, one of our heat pump experts will go to your house in person to verify the details, calculate the heating load, and make any final changes to the estimate.
Who is doing the installation?
One of Manatee's vetted, top-quality installers will handle the job. In 2025, we worked with about 4 such teams to handle ~75 installations. We handle all the paper work so pros can focus on what they do best. We typically dispatch installation teams closer to your local area whenever possible.
Do you front the Mass Save rebate?
If you are in Eversource & National Grid territory, we do take the Mass Save rebate off upfront so you don't have to deal with the complicated paperwork. Manatee's intake process collects all the documents we need, and we end up applying for the rebate and receiving it after approval. For those in MLP territories, you'll have to apply for the rebates yourself.
How much is the Mass Save rebate?
This year, in 2026, Mass Save rebates are $2,650/ton or whole home installations, with a cap of $8,500. They are expected to go down once again in 2027, as rebates slowly taper off over time. In addition to the rebate, the 0% HEAT Loan is still in place, and you can finance up to $25k with it.
What brand of heat pumps do you install?
In the past, we've installed Mitsubishi, Daikin, LG, and Midea. Part of the wide range is due to our research partnership with Laminar Collective, which publishes reviews on different types of heat pumps & our experience installing them. In 2026, we are leaning towards Daikin for ductless, and every other brand is in play for ducted installations.
Do you do multifamily/condo installations?
Usually we don't. But sometimes, during the winter off-season, we do. Typically, to get us to work on a condo, you'll need to arrange for 5+ units at the same condo to all get the installations done around the same time, or else it's just not worth the complexity. And you'll need to ensure that there's accessible balcony space for the condenser, that the electrical work is reasonable...etc. There's a lot to consider. If you're interested, I recommend signing up on Laminar Collective & reaching out about it that way.
